# **App Name**: Creati Growth Platform

## Core Features:

- Homepage Impact: Visually impactful homepage that conveys the value proposition: 'Turning Invisible Brands into Unforgettable Ones.' Features a clear call-to-action (CTA) for service discovery and consultation booking.
- About Us - Trust Builder: A 'not like any other agency' narrative that focuses on transparency, collaboration, and measurable impact. It highlights real-time progress visibility and the strategic rationale behind every design decision.
- Services Showcase: Categorized service offerings (Brand Identity, Digital Presence, Visual Marketing, Strategic Finance) with clear descriptions and CTAs to request tailored packages or initiate conversations.
- Testimonial Carousel: A rotating display of client testimonials, reinforcing trust and social proof with quotes emphasizing increased sales and brand satisfaction.
- Portfolio Display: Visually compelling portfolio of success stories, showcasing before/after transformations, social media feeds, logos, websites, and packaging examples.
- Contact & Conversion: Simple contact form integrated with WhatsApp and optional scheduling tool. Empathetic messaging that encourages initial contact: 'Transforming Visions into Brands'.
- Brand Diagnostic Quiz: Interactive quiz to diagnose brand challenges and provide tailored recommendations using a reasoning tool for customized results (Identity, Digital Presence, Marketing, Structure/Finance).

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A vibrant blue (#29ABE2) to convey trust, innovation, and approachability, reflecting the agency's modern and strategic approach.
- Background color: Light, desaturated blue (#E5F5FF) to provide a clean and professional backdrop, ensuring readability and focus.
- Accent color: A lively orange (#FFA500) as a contrasting accent to draw attention to calls-to-action and important interactive elements.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif for a modern and neutral look, suitable for headlines or body text
- Clean, minimalist icons that complement the overall aesthetic. Icons should use the primary blue color with the orange accent sparingly to highlight key interactive elements.
- Clean and structured layout, prioritizing visual hierarchy and ease of navigation. Ample white space should be used to give the design a modern and professional feel.
- Subtle animations and transitions to enhance user experience and create a sense of dynamism, particularly on the homepage and in the portfolio section.
